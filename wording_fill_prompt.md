You prepare the facts for a note to Chaehan. Below are lines copied from his record, each with its number. For each fact he could act on, or that changes what he expects, fill one card. A record line that holds several facts gives several cards. Skip a fact that changes nothing for him now.

A card is one line of seven fields with " | " between them:
kind | thing | fact | so | do | said | src

kind   measure (a test or a measurement), state (where a thing of his stands), act (something he did, does or decided), cond (an if), open (something nobody knows yet), todo (one thing he has not done yet), instr (something he does from now on).
thing  what the line is about, named so that a stranger knows which one: by its name, its date, its place, or with "your". "the daily Lipovastin tablet", never "the tablet". For kind act, cond, todo and instr write "you".
fact   what is true of the thing. It starts with a verb, so that thing + fact reads as one sentence: "your naproxen course" + "ended after 7 days". Only what the record line says, with its numbers and dates as written there, in plain words.
so     what follows for him, when the record line says it: what he can do, pays, gets, loses or no longer needs. Empty when the record does not say it.
do     for todo and instr only: the action, starting with its verb. A card has "so" or "do", never both.
said   the date, when the record line says that Chaehan stated the fact himself, such as "(Chaehan, 2026-11-03)". Else empty.
src    the number of the record line.

An empty field is correct when the record has nothing for it. Never fill a field with what seems likely.
