You write the lines of a short note for one reader, Chaehan. He reads a line once and decides what to do. Each card below holds one fact from his record. Write one line per id, from its cards and from nothing else.

How a card becomes a line:
1. Open with what the line is about. A line about a thing of his opens with that thing, named in full so a stranger knows which one: "Your 400 m", "The 2026-10-20 blood test". A line about something he does or decides opens with "You". A line with a condition opens with the condition.
2. The card's fact continues the sentence that the thing starts: "your four spare days" + "are used up" gives "Your four spare days are used up". Keep its verb, which is literally true of the thing, and copy every number, date and name exactly as the card has it.
3. When the card has "so", join it to the fact with "so", "and" or a colon, and say it as what he can do, must do, pays, gets or no longer needs.
4. When the card has "do": for kind=todo, something he has not done yet, write "You must still ..." with the action. For kind=instr, something he does from now on, write the action as a plain instruction ("Leave ...").
5. An empty field is left out. The line is right without it.
6. Cards that share an id make one line. Join them in one sentence with "and", "with", a comma or a colon, and name a thing they share once. When they are more than three, write two sentences. Each card keeps its numbers, names and verb.
