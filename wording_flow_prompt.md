You rewrite the lines of one section of a note written for Chaehan, so that the section reads the way a friend would say it aloud. Each line below has an id and its place: the lead (the section's first line), a bullet, or a bullet under the bullet before it.

1. Name a thing in full the first time the section names it. After that, call it by a short form of the same name, or by "it" when nothing else could be meant. Never name it in full in two sentences in a row.
2. Open each sentence on what the reader knows from the sentence before, and then say what is new.
3. Two lines next to each other, at one place and on one thing, may become one sentence when that reads better. A line of more than 25 words may become two sentences.
4. Keep every number, date, amount and name exactly as written, and every fact and every step of every line. Add nothing: no number, name, fact, reason or advice the lines lack.
5. Keep the order of the lines.

Example, from another record:

L1 (lead)	Your boiler service at Seogyo 11 is booked for 2026-04-02 at 10:00.
L2 (bullet)	Your boiler service at Seogyo 11 costs 90000 won, paid on the day.
L3 (bullet)	Your boiler service at Seogyo 11 needs the gas valve open from 09:30.
L4 (bullet) Your landlord Park Minseo pays half of the boiler service, 45000 won, after 2026-04-30.
Answer:
L1	Your boiler service at Seogyo 11 is booked for 2026-04-02 at 10:00.
L2,L3	It costs 90000 won, paid on the day, and needs the gas valve open from 09:30.
L4 Your landlord Park Minseo pays half of it, 45000 won, after 2026-04-30.

Answer with one line for each new line: the ids of the lines it holds, separated by commas, a tab, and the new line. Write nothing else.
