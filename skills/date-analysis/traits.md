## The few things most apparent in her

Pick three to five, the ones the date showed most clearly. For a Korean woman
in her late thirties on a matchmaking date, these usually decide most:

- **How she shows interest without saying it.** Offers of time, questions
  about his family, plans she makes unasked, teasing, 반말 slipping in. A Korean
  woman rarely says "I like you" by the third meeting; these acts say it.
- **How she sets her limits.** On touch, pace and sex, and how she says no:
  directly, by a joke, by changing the subject, by "좀 더 느껴볼게요".
- **What she expects of marriage and family.** Children, where to live, her
  parents' place in her life, the timeline she feels at 37.
- **How she handles fear and hurt.** Past relationships, being alone, being
  judged, and whether she tells him or manages it quietly.
- **How she decides.** Alone and by her own reasons, or by asking others.

Read form over content: how she says a thing tells more than what she claims.
A habit seen on all her meetings with him outweighs one vivid line, so name the
earlier meeting where it showed. A line said after several drinks says when.

## Reading indirect Korean about sex, relationships and children

A Korean woman on an early date rarely names sex. She says "open", "conservative",
"limit", "that kind of thing", and the topic comes from the turns around the
line. Decode it, quote it verbatim with its time, and give the reading in one
plain sentence. Search for these words in her turns and his:

| Word | Usual meaning on a date |
| --- | --- |
| 개방적, 오픈, 열려 있다 | open about sex or relationships |
| 보수적, 보수 | conservative about sex (sex before marriage, pace) |
| 리밋, 선, 선을 넘다, 진도 | how far and how fast she goes physically |
| 스킨십, 손잡기, 뽀뽀, 키스 | physical closeness |
| 육체적, 촉감, 몸, 잠자리, 속궁합, 자다, 같이 하다 | sex |
| 원나잇, 하룻밤, 여러 명이랑, 오픈 릴레이션쉽 | casual or multiple partners |
| 그런 거, 그런 게, 그 부분, 그쪽 | a topic too delicate to name: read the turns before |
| 연애, 남자친구, 사귀다, 없는 시간이 많았다, 오래 사귀다 | her relationship history |
| 결혼 전에, 결혼하면 | sex or children tied to marriage |
| 아이, 애기, 아기, 자녀, 킨더 (German Kinder), 낳다, 엄마 되다 | children |
